Important limitation
--------------------
The full real Ollama experiment cannot be executed in the assistant's Linux environment because the user's MCP/Ollama
runtime is a Windows-local environment. The scripts therefore perform package, pytest, data-hash, and Ollama preflight
checks on the user's machine before expensive trials start. Experimental model failures are valid outcomes and are not
converted into fake successes.

Data provenance rule
--------------------

- wikidata_presidential_record.csv
- hdata_plt_cleaned.csv
and stores SHA-256 hashes in redo_data/manifest.json.
