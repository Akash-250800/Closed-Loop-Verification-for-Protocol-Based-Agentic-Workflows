FINAL REDO RUNNER FIX

Root cause of the previous ExceptionGroup
-----------------------------------------
The previous seed-builder tried to obtain a perfect three-step LLM seed chain before
the real experiment. A schema-valid but semantically wrong reserve_stock proposal could
execute and mutate inventory state before the seed oracle rejected it. Later retries
then ran against already-changed state. When the builder exhausted its attempts, the
RuntimeError was raised inside the MCP async context and surfaced as an ExceptionGroup.

Final design
------------
1. No-fault research cells remain genuine end-to-end Qwen/Ollama runs.
2. Injected-fault research cells use canonical valid calls derived directly from the
   benchmark task specification as the common pre-fault starting point.
3. Canonical control calls are setup controls only; they are not counted as LLM outputs.
4. Naive retry replays the identical corrupted proposal.
5. Closed-loop GVEF receives structured feedback and asks Qwen to regenerate.
6. 30-country multi-reference controlled calls use balanced source order:
   15 Wikidata->H-DATA and 15 H-DATA->Wikidata.
7. Natural 30-country no-fault trials and all two-agent trials remain LLM-driven so
   agent source/tool choice is still measured.
8. All research output folders are new redo_v3_* folders. V1/V2 folders are untouched.
9. A deterministic pre-run mechanism gate runs before the expensive research matrix:
      transient_api + naive_retry must recover
      type_mismatch + naive_retry must fail
   If this gate fails, the script aborts before the 1,268-row run.
10. ExceptionGroup errors are now expanded to show their real sub-exceptions.
